How much of this draft survives an edit?

Judge the draft as written. The question is how much of it has to change.

- `reject` — less than half survives: the draft has to be written again. A fact is wrong, a claim is unsupported, or the structure breaks the reader's understanding.
- `revise` — most of it survives: the outline, the order and the voice stay, but some sections are wrong, unsupported, or missing.
- `pass` — it ships as it stands; an editor would change wording, not substance.
