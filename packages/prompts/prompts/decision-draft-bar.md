Which of the three outcomes does this draft's own review land on?

Read the findings. The review's own verdict follows from the severity of what it found.

- `reject` — a Critical finding: a fact is wrong, a claim is unsupported, or the structure is broken. The draft has to be written again.
- `revise` — Major or Moderate findings, and no Critical one: the draft is sound but parts of it are wrong, unsupported, or missing. A revision pass fixes it.
- `pass` — nothing worse than Minor findings: the draft ships as it stands.

A Major finding means "this part must be fixed". On its own it does not mean "rewrite the draft".
