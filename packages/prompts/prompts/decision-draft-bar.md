Which of the three outcomes did this draft's review produce?

Read the findings, then apply the review's own gate.

- `reject` — a Critical finding: a fact is wrong, a claim is unsupported, or the structure is broken. The draft has to be written again.
- `revise` — the review did not pass the draft, and there is no Critical finding: the draft is sound but parts of it are wrong, unsupported, or missing. A revision pass fixes it.
- `pass` — the review passed the draft. It ships as it stands.

The gate is the review's own: a draft the review passed ships, whatever the risk flags say. A Major finding means "this part must be fixed" — on its own it does not mean "rewrite the draft", and it does not stop a draft the review passed.
