Does this render finding have to stop the release?

Judge the finding as it is described, not as it might be.

- `block` — the rendered video is unusable as published: text is unreadable, a required asset is missing, or the video contradicts what it claims to show.
- `review` — a human should look before publishing, but the video is still watchable: a scene boundary was missed, a caption is dense, a scene ran together.
- `pass` — the finding is cosmetic or informational, and the video can ship unchanged.
