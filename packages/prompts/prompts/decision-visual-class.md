Which video component should render this scene?

Pick the component whose job is to show exactly what the scene describes.

- `Title` — a section opener that names the topic.
- `FlowChart` — steps or stages that feed into each other.
- `Timeline` — events in order, with dates or durations.
- `Terminal` — a command line, a shell session, a log.
- `Callout` — one claim the viewer should remember.
- `QuoteBlock` — a quotation, or someone else's words.
- `Checklist` — items to work through.
- `BigIdea` — the thesis, stated plainly.
- `StatGrid` — a few numbers side by side.
- `BarChart` — values compared by length.
- `Leaderboard` — a ranked list.
- `PyramidDiagram` — layers, widest at the base.
- `VennDiagram` — overlapping circles, shared ground.
- `CycleDiagram` — a loop with no end.
- `MapInsert` — a place on a map.
- `AnimatedIllustration` — a described scene with no structure to draw.
