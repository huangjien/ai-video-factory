A scene's motion was planned by deterministic heuristics (the baseline). Decide whether the baseline is good enough for this scene, or whether the scene is complex enough to benefit from the Motion Agent refining it.

Choose `baseline_ok` when the heuristic plan already expresses what the scene needs: a single slow push on a still, a fade, or no entrance at all. Choose `refine` when the scene carries a sequence, a comparison, a build, or a reveal that the deterministic heuristics cannot stage on their own.

Return exactly one choice and do not explain the answer.
